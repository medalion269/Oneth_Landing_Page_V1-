## Architecture
- `app/page.tsx` sets the section order: Navbar, Hero, DocumentWorkflow, Reconciliation, ApprovalWorkflow, PortalScene, Features, CapacityCheck, PilotForm, Footer, MobileTabBar, ScrollManager.
- This app's root is `landing/` (its own package.json, config and docs). The "Sign in" links go to the app at `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` + `/login` (`lib/app-url.ts`): in development they default to http://localhost:5173, and a production build without the variable hides them.
- `lib/animations.ts` holds every scroll timeline, one function per section. Each uses `gsap.matchMedia()` with three modes:
  - **desktop** (>=1024px with motion): pinned, scrubbed, reversible timelines
  - **mobile** (<1024px with motion): the phone-specific scenes
  - **reduced motion**: nothing animates
- Rule: markup is always rendered in its finished state, and timelines animate from earlier states. Reduced-motion and no-JS visitors must still see the complete story. Keep this rule.
- Pinned desktop scenes are drawn on a fixed design canvas (`components/Stage.tsx`, 1200 x 600) that scales to fit. Positions are measured with `offsetWithin()`, which ignores transforms.
- The hero's load-in is pure CSS (`.word-mask`, `.anim-rise`, `.hero-dash-enter`). The dashboard's starting tilt lives in CSS (`.hero-dash`), and GSAP takes over from the same values.
- **Hero on phones and tablets** (below 1024px): the copy is editorial and left-aligned (a "For UAE accounting firms" chip, the headline larger, one Apply button, "See how it works" as a link, the pilot's terms as three ticks; `docs/DECISIONS.md`, L21). Below it, a silent looping film of the desktop app (`HeroFilm.tsx`, `public/hero-film.mp4`, made in `hero-film/`, kept local): fetched only below 1024px and without reduced motion, started whenever that layout appears, so a turned tablet or a narrowed window plays it too (L19), and cropped to the band every scene uses, 8:9, for less empty space (L20). From 1024px the hero is unchanged.
- **Hero, "do the work yourself"** (desktop): once the app lies flat it holds for about a screen of scrolling (pin 240%). The first time a visitor scrolls down onto it, an invite card offers "Clear Saffron Grove's to-do list" (Start / No thanks). It stays until one of them is clicked: scrolling past the hero and back, or a resize, leaves it up; it is only out of sight while the app is tilted above its flat pose and cannot be clicked (L28). On Start the guide (`hero-app/Guide.tsx`) holds the page and points at one click at a time: the lit target pulses, everything else is dimmed and can't be clicked (four blockers around the spotlight plus a capture-phase click guard), and a short card says what is happening. The visitor does the work; oneth's part (reading the invoice, proposing the next one, the client answering, reading the photo) happens in response (`hero-app/useStory.ts`). Skip, Escape, a resize, 30 s without input or the scroll scene going away stop it; the pill beside the title then says "Continue the tasks" (or "Try it · 3 tasks", "Start over"). It never invites from a restored scroll position, a link jump past the hero (`isNavigating`) or on the way back up. With reduced motion: no invite, no hold, the pill runs it, the client's answer arrives at once. `heroAnimation` keeps the app `inert` until it is flat, hides the landing's top bar while it fills the pinned screen, and sends `HERO_EVENTS` (live, arrive, cancel). Phones and tablets keep the compact two-card picture (starting counts).
- The form posts to `app/api/apply/route.ts`, with validation shared through `lib/validation.ts`.
  - Delivery, first one configured wins: Supabase if `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` are set, otherwise `SUBMISSION_ENDPOINT`, otherwise an email per submission through Resend (`RESEND_API_KEY` + `RESEND_TO`, optional `RESEND_FROM`; plain text, replies go to the visitor).
  - With none set it runs in **development mode**: nothing is saved and the page says so. See `.env.example` and the SQL in `README.md`.
